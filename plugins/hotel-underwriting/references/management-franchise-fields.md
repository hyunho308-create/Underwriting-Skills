# Management and Franchise Agreement Fields

Read the applicable section for each agreement type being extracted. Retain only material, source-supported provisions and route them to the indicated information primitive.

## Management agreements

Use `property.manager` for:

- Parties, guarantors, manager identity, commencement, term, expiration, renewal, and transition.
- Base fee; incentive fee, profit measure, owner-return hurdle, reserve deduction, exclusions, caps, and other manager fees.
- FF&E or replacement reserves, operator funding, key money, guarantees, reimbursements, amortization, repayment, and forgiveness.
- Performance tests, measurement periods, thresholds, exclusions, cure rights, and remedies.
- Transfer and termination rights, lockouts, notice and cure, purchaser qualifications, fees or damages, repayments, and transition duties.
- Employer and payroll responsibility, liquor-license holder, lender non-disturbance, and standstill rights.
- Consent rights, non-competes or area restrictions, annual-plan limits, third-party F&B, trademarks, ESG obligations, and other material operating constraints.

Keep manager, brand, owner, employer, license holder, and trademark owner distinct. Do not infer non-disturbance merely because an SNDA exists.

## Franchise agreements

Use `property.brand` for:

- Franchisor, licensor, brand or collection, franchisee, owner, guarantor, conversion, opening or authorization, term, expiration, and renewal.
- Franchise fee and exact revenue basis, ladders, step-ups, reductions, and effective periods.
- Marketing, program, reservation, loyalty, technology, training, procurement, quality-assurance, transaction, and other brand charges.
- FF&E reserves, franchisor investment, key money, PIP funding, payment timing, amortization, repayment, and forgiveness.
- Liquidated damages, termination and transfer rights, notice and cure, purchaser qualification, competitor premiums, and de-identification duties.
- PIP scope, deadlines, extensions, cost responsibility, conversion conditions, and consequences of noncompletion.
- Brand standards, protected area, guaranties, trademarks, lender or owner-agreement conditions, opening deadlines, and other material constraints.

Keep franchisor, brand, collection, manager, owner, guarantor, and trademark owner distinct. Keep FF&E reserves, PIP obligations, and mandated capital spending separate.
